# MusicTutor - what changed

## 0.1.0

The first version under the MusicTutor name.

**Tutorial**
- Build a lesson scene from cameras, a keyboard, a notation staff, a chord
  readout, a note display, a bass neck, text and images, in landscape,
  portrait and square. Two scenes, PIANO and BASS, are there to start from.
- Record video and MIDI together, and go live to several services at once,
  in two shapes at the same time if you want.
- A backing-track player with tempo detection, looping by bars, slowing down
  and transposing, and a metronome that can stay out of the recording.
- Bass mode: play a bass and see the note, where it sits on the neck, and
  what it is in the key.

**Learn**
- Open a song or a video to see its chords and its notes on a piano roll,
  then loop, slow down and transpose any part of it.
- Separate a song into bass, keys, guitar, vocals, drums and the rest. Mute
  the part you are learning and play along with the band. A separated song is
  kept, so it only takes the time once.
- Watch YouTube inside the app with the chord named as it plays, on keys, a
  bass neck or a guitar neck. Record a video's sound to study it properly.
- Share a lesson: a recording and the MIDI played in it travel as one file,
  and whoever opens it sees the exact notes that were played.

**Where things are kept**
- Your lessons and recordings are in Documents, Amanorsac Studio, MusicTutor.
  Anything made with an earlier build is copied there the first time this one
  starts; nothing is deleted from where it was.
